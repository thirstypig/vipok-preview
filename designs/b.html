How the system works

Three steps, one chart

No lab calculations and no waiting on reorders. The reference chart turns two measurements into a lens code.

01

Measure K

Take flat and steep K. Mean K picks the chart row, from A (flattest) to ZE (steepest).

Mean K = (flat + steep) / 2
02

Select from the chart

Spherical equivalent picks the column, rounded down. The row and column give the lens code.

Example: 43.56 D, −4.44 D → 2O06
03

Dispense and fine-tune

Dispense from your inventory set. To adjust the fit, move up or down rows in the same column.

Target: bullseye pattern

For patients and parents

What is orthokeratology?

Ortho-k uses rigid gas permeable lenses, worn overnight, to gradually reshape the cornea. You take them out in the morning and see clearly without glasses or daytime contacts.

VIPOK patients are cared for by certified, trained practitioners. Every lens is individually shrink-wrapped; ask to see the original package before you take lenses home.
