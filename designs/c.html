Questions

What makes VIPOK lenses different?

Each VIPOK practitioner controls the fit, so your doctor can fine-tune and adjust for the right fit. That means better results, fewer complications and more comfort.

How do I know my doctor is giving me genuine VIPOK lenses?

Every lens dispensed by a certified VIPOK doctor is individually shrink-wrapped. Ask to see the original package before you take your lenses home.

Is ortho-k safe?

Safety depends on proper fitting, lens care and follow-up visits with your eye doctor. [Safety and warning language to be supplied by VIPOK regulatory]
